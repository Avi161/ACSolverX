# C15 stall: BS(m,m+1) with companion `YXXXyxYx`

Not a theorem. A named target.

On the best table, ten rows are

```
⟨ YXXXyxYx ,  Y^{m+1} x^{±1} y^m x^{∓1} ⟩
```

for m=3..7 and both inner signs. After rotation the second relator is
consecutive BS(m,m+1). The donor has x-exponent −1. Britton still fails:
conjugation by the stable letter implements `y^m ↦ y^{m+1}`, so length-1
y-syllables in the donor (`Y … y … Y`) are not Britton-reducible.

Whitehead minima (from the wave-2 census): donor 7, BS relator = its
cyclic length. Neither is primitive, so C12 does not fire.

A legal continuation must do one of:

- change the donor by AC1–AC3 so every y-syllable length is a multiple of
  m, then rerun Britton as a proof not a 10k search;
- exhibit an AK3-style relator `a v^{-k}` with `gcd(k,m)=1` and a defining
  power, expanded into AC moves;
- stably change generators so one relator becomes primitive (C12), with
  an explicit Aut witness — already ruled out for the present spellings.

Do not repeat the residual supermove Britton preflight. Do not declare
triviality from BS(1,2) symmetry or from reciprocal BS(3,2).
