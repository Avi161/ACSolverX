# Euclidean marked kernels for a power donor

The construction below is a finite stable AC equivalence on known trivial-group inputs. It gives an explicit new marked frame for the swapped `aca_59` power donor, but no shorter certified presentation. The companion JSON contains a directly checked basis, its inverse, all20 domain Nielsen operations, and a separate16-image audit of the root's ordinary-search endpoint. No heap search was run for this note.

## General construction

Let

    P=(R(x,y),S(x,y)),       S=y^-d x^n,
    f:F(r,s,t,u)->F(x,y),   f(r)=R, f(s)=S, f(t)=x, f(u)=y^q,

where d>0, n is any integer and gcd(d,q)=1. Replace s by A=s t^-n. This is a free-basis change fixing r,t,u, and f(A)=y^-d. The two basis elements A,u have image exponent vector (-d,q). Apply the ordinary integer Euclidean algorithm using elementary row additions, inversions and swaps, recording exactly the same Nielsen operations on the words A,u. It terminates at an ordered pair (K,T) with images (1,y). No commutation of A and u is used: integer arithmetic is valid for their images because those images lie in the cyclic group generated by y. The recorded word operations preserve the domain free basis throughout.

Now t,T have images x,y. Put

    W1=r R(t,T)^-1,        W2=K.

Replacing r by W1 is again a free-basis change, so B=(W1,K,t,T) is a free basis with f(B)=(1,1,x,y). Let pi kill the original domain letters r,s and relabel t,u as x,y. Write

    Tbar=pi(T),            Kbar=pi(K).

The projected pair pi(W1),pi(K), after inverting its first relator, is

    Q=(R(x,Tbar),Kbar).

This proves that every such coprime power choice admits a deterministic marked-kernel construction. It does not guarantee shorter words or a different ambient orbit. Changing the Euclidean choices changes the marking and may change the literal Q; record the full word path, not merely the final exponent matrix.

## Why this is stable AC, not just a quotient identification

Because B is a free basis with the displayed f-images, ker f is the normal closure of W1,K. Consequently the rank4 tuple

    E=(r,s,W1,K)

presents the same trivial group as P. This establishes E's triviality before any stable ambient-coordinate lemma is invoked. Killing the original unit relators r,s in E gives pi(W1),pi(K), and hence Q after a row inversion. Each deletion from another row is an elementary unit-donor AC insertion/removal.

In the other direction, the inverse-basis automorphism theta sends W1,K to the first two coordinate unit letters. Apply theta to E and use those units to clear the first two rows. The remaining literal pair is P in the marked t,T coordinates. On known trivial-group tuples, the accepted stable substitution-and-removal lemma realizes each elementary ambient Nielsen step by finitely many stable AC moves with one reusable helper. Thus P and Q are stably AC equivalent. This does not assert ordinary AC equivalence, and it does not assume the AC conjecture.

For completeness, the normal-product point underlying the accepted lemma is essential. In a known trivial presentation, every defining word w is a finite product of conjugates of the old relators and their inverses. Stabilize with the fresh unit z, invert it, and append that product while restoring each old donor to obtain Z w. Conversely, after a defining-generator substitution the remaining tuple still presents the trivial group; express its defining word as a finite normal product of those remaining relators, clear that word from the defining relator, and delete the resulting unit. These are finite AC composites, although an existence proof does not supply a numerical bound on the expanded word or move lengths. The inverse-basis bridge above uses four displayed generators and may need a fifth for these ambient composites.

## Closed formula for odd d and q=2

For d=2h+1 with h>=1, the Euclidean algorithm takes the particularly short form

    A=s t^-n,
    H=A u^h,             f(H)=y^-1,
    K=u H^2,             f(K)=1,
    T=H^-1,              f(T)=y.

All operations are elementary Nielsen operations expanded h or2 times as appropriate. Projection gives

    Tbar=y^-h x^n,
    Kbar=y (x^-n y^h)^2,
    Q=(R(x,y^-h x^n), y (x^-n y^h)^2).

For d=5 this is precisely `Tbar=y^-2 x^n`, `Kbar=y(x^-n y^2)^2`. For d=7 it is `Tbar=y^-3 x^n`, `Kbar=y(x^-n y^3)^2`. The proof concerns words in a free group; replacing A,u by commuting formal symbols would destroy the needed basis certificate.

An explicit inverse of B=(W1,K,t,T), written using positional coordinate letters r,s,t,u, is

    old r = r R(t,u),
    old s = u^-1 (s u^2)^-h t^n,
    old t = t,
    old u = s u^2.

Substitution verifies both inverse compositions. In particular, killing the first two new coordinates in the inverse image of old s gives y^-(2h+1) x^n exactly.

## Exact aca_59 certificate

The original pair is

    (YXXYxxyxx, YYYYxxxxxxx), total20.

Invert the second relator and swap the two ambient axes. The working pair is

    (XYYXyyxyy, YYYYYYYxxxx), total20,

so d=7,n=4,q=2,h=3. Our marking has

    A=sTTTT,
    H=sTTTTuuu,
    T=UUUttttS,
    K=usTTTTuuusTTTTuuu.

The complete W1 and all20 Nielsen operations are in the companion JSON. Both inverse compositions and f(B)=(1,1,x,y) were independently checked. The inverse basis is particularly short:

    (rTUUTuutuu, UUUSUUSUUStttt, t, suu).

Our raw projected pair is

    (XXXXXyyyXXXXyyyXYYYxxxxYYYxxxxxYYYxxxxYYYxxxx,
     yXXXXyyyXXXXyyy), total60.

This is a finite stable frame, not a gain. All relators are counted at the explicit macro boundaries: P plus two units has length22; E has length71; theta(E) has length26; Q plus two units has length62. These values do not bound the unexpanded elementary stable intermediates.

## Exact ambient classification of that endpoint

At the root's explicit request, this note evaluates the eight elementary Nielsen maps on the displayed endpoint, cyclically reducing each row. These are16 additional word-image evaluations **after** the root's shared1000-unit attempt; they are not claimed to fit inside its budget. No strict reduction is accepted because every map increases total length:

| Changed generator image | Fixed generator | Cyclic total |
|---|---|---:|
| xy, xY, yx, Yx | y |25 in each case|
| yx, yX, xy, Xy | x |22 in each case|

The JSON retains every exact raw image and cyclic reduction. In rank2, the nontrivial Whitehead moves, modulo signed permutations and inner automorphisms, are precisely these Nielsen maps. Signed permutations preserve cyclic length, as do inner automorphisms. Whitehead's length-reduction theorem for finite tuples of cyclic words therefore proves that this endpoint has minimum cyclic total21 in its ambient orbit; see [Whitehead, On Certain Sets of Elements in a Free Group (1936)](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s2-41.1.48).

It follows that the displayed endpoint is outside the ambient orbit of the original pair, which already has length20. No independent proof of the original pair's minimality is needed for that separation. This is a genuinely different ambient frame, but still worse in total length. The ordinary search may itself change ambient orbits, so this endpoint calculation alone does not classify the raw projected pair's ambient orbit. No universal claim that all Euclidean power complements escape or return is justified.
