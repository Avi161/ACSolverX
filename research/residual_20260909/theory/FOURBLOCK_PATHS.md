# Four-block roots in the AC19 census: population, structure and certified paths

Scope: `results/heuristic_search/ac19_final_policy_full_1k/rows_*.jsonl`
(72,779 rows, 72,052 solved), read only. Mining code
[`FOURBLOCK_mine.py`](FOURBLOCK_mine.py); machine output
[`FOURBLOCK_mine.json`](FOURBLOCK_mine.json), example paths
[`FOURBLOCK_examples.jsonl`](FOURBLOCK_examples.jsonl), bounded probe
[`FOURBLOCK_probe.json`](FOURBLOCK_probe.json). Reproduce with

```bash
PYTHONPATH=. python3 research/residual_20260909/theory/FOURBLOCK_mine.py          # ~13 s
PYTHONPATH=. python3 research/residual_20260909/theory/FOURBLOCK_mine.py --probe  # 40 rows
```

Nothing under `research/supermoves_20260908/`, `results/` or `data/` was
written. No search was run except the explicitly bounded probe of section 7
(40 rows, at most 2,132 charged children per row).

**Definition.** A *four-block* relator is a cyclically reduced word with
exactly four maximal generator runs, i.e. `y^a x^b y^c x^d` with
`a,b,c,d != 0`. A *four-block root* is a canonical root pair (`canon_pair` of
the census `pair`) at least one of whose relators is four-block.

## 2. The exponent patterns `(a,b,c,d)` up to symmetry

The symmetry group used throughout is generated by

| generator | action on `(a,b,c,d)` | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `sigma` | `(b,c,d,a)` | the `x <-> y` relabelling, then rotate to a y-block |
| `sigma^2` | `(c,d,a,b)` | rotation of the block cycle |
| `iota` | `(-c,-b,-a,-d)` | relator inversion |
| `eps_x` | `(a,-b,c,-d)` | `x -> X` |
| `eps_y` | `(-a,b,-c,d)` | `y -> Y` |

(32 elements; `sigma`, `eps_x`, `eps_y` are signed permutations of the basis —
legitimate ambient transport under T2 — and `sigma^2`, `iota` are exactly what
`canon_rel` already quotients by.) Canonical representative = the
lexicographically least tuple in the orbit. **215 distinct canonical tuples**
occur among the 43,746 solved four-block roots.

| canonical `(a,b,c,d)` | rows | family (sec. 3) | Aut-orbit class (sec. 4) |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `(-2,-1, 1, 1)` | 18,837 | `bs_stable_s1` | irreducible, 4 blocks |
| `(-2,-1, 1,-1)` | 6,740 | `merge_pm1` | two-block `y^-2 x^-3` (trefoil) |
| `(-2,-2,-1, 1)` | 3,110 | `generic` | irreducible, 4 blocks |
| `(-2,-2, 1, 1)` | 2,995 | `generic` | irreducible, 4 blocks |
| `(-2,-1,-1,-1)` | 2,377 | `merge_pm1` | **primitive** |
| `(-2,-2, 1, 2)` | 1,247 | `bs_stable_s2` | irreducible, 4 blocks (`YYXXyxx`) |
| `(-3,-1, 2, 1)` | 867 | `bs_stable_s1` | irreducible, 4 blocks |
| `(-3,-2, 1, 1)` | 749 | `generic` | irreducible, 4 blocks |
| `(-3,-2, 2, 1)` | 552 | `generic` | irreducible, 4 blocks |
| `(-3,-2, 1,-1)` | 537 | `generic` | irreducible, 4 blocks |
| `(-3,-2,-1, 1)` | 518 | `generic` | irreducible, 4 blocks |
| `(-2,-2,-2,-1)` | 367 | `generic` | two-block `y^-2 x^-3` |

The top canonical tuple, `(-2,-1,1,1)`, **is** the consecutive-BS(1,2) donor
`YXXyx` — a BS(m,m+1) relator is a four-block word, so T5's whole population is
a sub-family here.

### Exponent sums

The unordered pair `{|exp_x|, |exp_y|}` is invariant under the whole symmetry
group, and `|det| = 1` forces `gcd(|exp_x|,|exp_y|) = 1`.

| `{|ex|,|ey|}` | rows | | `{|ex|,|ey|}` | rows |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| `{0,1}` | **21,283** | | `{1,4}` | 1,011 |
| `{1,2}` | 8,060 | | `{3,4}` | 839 |
| `{1,3}` | 3,780 | | `{2,5}` | 580 |
| `{1,1}` | 3,646 | | `{1,5}` | 464 |
| `{2,3}` | 3,284 | | rest | < 250 each |

**`{0,k}` occurs only with `k = 1` — 21,283 times, never once with `k >= 2`.**
That is not sampling: it is forced (Lemma L1, proved in
[`FOURBLOCK_THEORY.md`](FOURBLOCK_THEORY.md) sec. 3, verified 21,565/21,565 on
the census and 91/91 on the residual).

population and **70 % of the residual**.

## 4. What an ambient Nielsen map does to a four-block relator

Minimum block count of the four images `x->xy, x->xY, y->yx, y->yX`:

| min image blocks | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 | 14 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rows | **9,502** | 24,550 | 8,670 | 753 | 242 | 26 | 3 |

and split by family:

| family | min image blocks = 2 | = 4 | >= 6 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `merge_pm1` | **9,502** | 683 | 0 |
| `bs_stable_s1` | 0 | 19,734 | 0 |
| `generic` | 0 | 4,133 | 8,145 |
| `bs_stable_s>=2` | 0 | 0 | 1,549 |

**A single Nielsen map lowers the block count of a four-block relator only in
the `merge_pm1` family, never anywhere else.** (Theory: `y -> y x^k` maps
`y^a x^b y^c x^d` blockwise only when `|a| = |c| = 1`; otherwise `(y x^k)^a`
splits into `|a|` separate y-blocks — see `FOURBLOCK_THEORY.md` sec. 2, which
also corrects the "shear" formula `y^a x^{b+ka} y^c x^{d+kc}` that only holds
abelianly.) The 683 `merge_pm1` rows with min image = 4 are those where the
merge needs `|k| >= 2`, i.e. `|k|` copies of the same map.

On the certified paths the *observed* behaviour is even sharper. Of the 410
solved rows where the first thing to change the four-block relator was an
ambient Nielsen map, the image had

| image blocks | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 18 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rows | 143 | 130 | 67 | 42 | 14 | 10 | 4 |

— **never 2 or 4.** No certified census path ever used an automorphism to turn
a four-block relator into a two-block or BS relator. Answering the brief's
question directly: *no*, the Nielsen maps do not do this in practice, and
provably cannot except in `merge_pm1`.

### Aut-orbit class (Whitehead-minimal form)

`orbit_class` explores the four Nielsen maps plus the three signed permutations
under a non-increasing cyclic-length rule and reports the least block count
attained at minimal length:

| orbit class | rows | interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| irreducible, 4 blocks | 33,193 (75.9 %) | no automorphism shortens or thins it |
| two-block `y^-2 x^-3` | 7,277 | torus/trefoil relator `T(2,3)` |
| **primitive** | **2,722** | pair is immediately AC-trivial (rule F1) |
| two-block `y^-2 x^-5` | 507 | `T(2,5)` |
| two-block `y^-2 x^-(7,9,11)` | 46 | `T(2,k)` |
| two-block `y^-3 x^-4` | 1 | `T(3,4)` |

On the residual: 281 of 293 are irreducible 4-block, 12 are `T(2,k)`
(`k` = 3, 5, 7), **0 are primitive**.

## 5. How the certified paths treat the four-block relator

### 5.1 Donor, not target

Role of the four-block relator at the **first** step of the certified path:

| family | donor `D` | target `T` | automorphism `A` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `bs_stable_s1` | 19,719 (99.9 %) | 15 | 0 |
| `merge_pm1` | 10,111 (99.3 %) | 52 | 19 |
| `generic` | 10,522 (85.7 %) | 1,453 | 284 |
| `bs_stable_s2` | 1,049 (73.6 %) | 325 | 49 |
| `bs_stable_s3/4/5` | 28 | 78 | 17 |

So: **the four-block relator is overwhelmingly consumed as a donor**, and the
larger the stable run the more often it is itself rewritten instead (the
`bs_stable_s>=3` rows invert that ratio completely — 78 targets against 28
donors — which is the first quantitative sign that the stable-power families
are the ones the policy cannot use as donors).

### 5.2 Steps until it changes, and donor uses before then

| family | first change: median / p90 / max | donor uses before it changes: median / mean |
|---|---|---|
| `bs_stable_s1` | 6 / 19 / 36 | 6 / 5.9 |

### 6.2 The moves themselves — the four-block row subtraction

Encoding a substitution `(target, jsign, k1, k2)` as `<sign><k1><k2>` with `0`
for "no rotation" and `r` for "some rotation", the prefixes of length 1-4 that
reach a gate are dominated by one move shape:

| gate | move-shape prefix | rows |
|---|---|---:|
| `one_occurrence` | `-00` | **895** |
| `one_occurrence` | `-00 -00` | 479 |
| `bs_accept` | `-00` | **446** |
| `one_occurrence` | `-rr` | 276 |
| `one_occurrence` | `-rr -00` | 271 |
| `one_occurrence` | `-r0` | 209 |
| `bs_accept` | `-rr` | 200 |
| `bs_accept` | `-00 -00` | 125 |
| `bs_accept` | `aut` | 92 |

`-00` is the move `r_j <- r_j · r_i^{-1}` with **no rotation at all** — exactly
the two-block row subtraction of `primitive_patterns.md` sec. 1, applied to
four-block words. One or two un-rotated donor subtractions is the single most
common bridge from a four-block root to a proved gate: at least 1,945 rows
reach a gate through a prefix consisting only of `-00` moves.

## 7. A bounded deterministic probe (40 rows)

Does a *deterministic* bridge exist? `gate_probe` enumerates every substitution
child, keeps the three shortest, tests the gates, and repeats once — charging
one unit per generated child (max 2,132 units on any single row here; 40 rows
total, 20 census-solved four-block roots that are not already gated and 20 dev
panel rows).

| population | rows | closed at depth <= 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| solved census four-block roots (not already gated) | 20 | **6** |
| residual dev-panel rows with a four-block relator | 20 | **1** (`ac19_109`, at depth 0) |

The single dev hit is the already-known `bs_accept_budget_starved` row: its root
*is* a BS-accept pair, so the probe closes it at depth 0 with 0 units. That
reconfirms the audit's finding that `ac19_109` is lost to staging, not to
mathematics, and it is the only residual dev row a shortest-first two-step probe
reaches a proved gate on.

Honest conclusion: **greedy shortest-child probing is not the bridge.** It
recovers 30 % of solved four-block roots and essentially nothing on the
residual. The bridges that work are the algebraic ones of
[`FOURBLOCK_THEORY.md`](FOURBLOCK_THEORY.md).

## 8. Summary of the data findings

1. Four-block roots are **60.7 % of all census solves** and **293 of the 727
   residual** — the largest structural family, confirming the audit's count
   exactly.
2. The population splits into four symmetry-invariant families; the one
   already covered by a theorem (`bs_stable_s1` = T5's BS donor) is 45 % of the
   solved population but only 8 of the 293 residual four-block roots.
3. `{|exp_x|,|exp_y|} = {0,k}` occurs **only** with `k = 1`, 21,283 times,
   never otherwise — consecutivity of the BS shape is forced by `|det| = 1`,
   not a hypothesis (Lemma L1).
4. A Nielsen map thins a four-block relator only in the `merge_pm1` family;
   on certified paths it *never* produced a two-block or four-block image.
5. 2,722 solved roots have a **primitive** four-block relator — those pairs are
   AC-trivial at the root by T3, and the census spent a median of 263 search
   units on each of them. 0 residual roots are primitive.
6. 7,831 solved roots have a four-block relator Aut-equivalent to a torus
   two-block word `y^2 x^k`; the unimplemented T6 is the theorem for those. On
   the residual this class is 12 rows.
7. The four-block relator acts as **donor** first in 86-99.9 % of rows
   (family-dependent), and the most frequent bridge to a proved gate is one or
   two *un-rotated* donor subtractions `r_j <- r_j r_i^{-1}` — the four-block
   analogue of the two-block row operation.
