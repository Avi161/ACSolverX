# Retained high-rank presentations with short individual relators

All 124 inputs are retained at rank 7–12, with every relator of length at most 3. All original and defining relators are counted; no automatic destabilization is performed. This universal representation change is not a trivialization or evidence of easier search.

The construction uses shared two-letter definitions. Replacing a recurring pair by a fresh generator adds its defining relator of length 3. The sum of max(0, row length − 3) strictly decreases, proving termination. Exact signed words and replayable composite events are in HIGH_RANK_TRIANGLES.json.

Construction cost: 12,234 named algebraic units; 0.12465 CPU seconds and 0.12500 search wall seconds, excluding verification and cooldown. Each row uses at most 1,000 units. These units are not heap pops.
