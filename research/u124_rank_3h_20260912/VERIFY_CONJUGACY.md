# Opposite-sign conjugacy templates: PASS

An exact template R=w U w^-1 V exists precisely when the nonempty U is
conjugate to red(R V^-1). Write U=s C s^-1 and the target=t D t^-1 with
cyclically reduced C,D. If C=A B and D=B A, then b=t A^-1 s^-1 is an exact
base conjugator. The implementation's recorded cyclic frames and rotation
cut reconstruct this b, and independent reduction verifies b U b^-1=target.

If r is the primitive root of U, all conjugators are b r^k. Indeed b^-1 w
centralizes U; a nontrivial free-group element acts by translation on its
unique tree axis, and its centralizer consists of powers of the smallest
positive translation preserving that axis. This is the cyclic centralizer
generated by r. The frame s must also conjugate the primitive root: for
U=11·7²·11^-1, r is 11·7·11^-1, not the bare generator7.

Introducing a helper for w gives the exact opposite-sign template
`h U h^-1 V`. Inverse deduplication reverses both helper signs. The independent
dispatcher checks every cyclic frame, root power and primitive-period test,
integer centralizer shift, helper sign, forced row, isolated-generator count,
all retained relators, and the full expanded word identity.

The controls verify51 short known conjugators against their stated cosets,
seven signed shifts with a noncyclic source, and inverse-commutator rejection.
A complete planted event passes native and JSON replay; six corrupted
metadata variants are rejected. Its pair (R,aR), with R=[a,b]a[a,b]^-1 b,
is independently known trivial because it first gives a=1 and then b=1.

The conjugator coset description is complete for a fixed equation. Discovery
tests only selected contexts, orientations and centralizer shifts within its
budget, so this is not complete template or stable-AC exploration. The empty
source is explicitly excluded because its centralizer is the whole group.
No algebraic bug was found; no census search was run.

Reproduce with `verification_conjugacy_checks.py`; the event and source hashes
are retained in `verification_conjugacy.json`.
