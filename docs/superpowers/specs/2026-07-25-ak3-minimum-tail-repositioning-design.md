# AK(3) minimum-tail repositioning design

Date: 2026-07-25

## Objective

Decide whether the first evaluated cross equation closes the two
minimum quotient-\(B\) length-six classes left by Result 25.

## Outcome

It does not close them.

The four previously displayed \(C_0,\rho\) representatives fail the exact
first-cross conjugacy test for every remaining conjugator. An exhaustive
relative-axis overcensus proves that their product lengths never equal
the required length six.

That statement is representative-specific because \(e=pb^{-1}\) keeps
\(p\) fixed. Repositioning \(C,\rho,b\) changes \(d=t^{-1}exe^{-1}\)
non-equivariantly. An explicit repositioned candidate in row
\((+,-,-)\) satisfies all three evaluated equations, has a non-braid
killer survivor, and occupies the minimum length-six class.

## Two-evaluation test

For

\[
\Phi=(r_e,r_p):G*\langle z\rangle\to G\times G,
\]

the exact image is

\[
\operatorname{im}\Phi
=
\{(g,gn):n\in\langle\!\langle e^{-1}p\rangle\!\rangle_G\}.
\]

For the candidate, the quotient by \(b=e^{-1}p\) is \(C_2\), so the
normal closure of \(b\) is precisely the even-weight subgroup. This lets
the evaluated bridges interpolate to the exact quotient-\(B\) witness.

## Remaining equation

With \(q=ze^{-1}\), the evaluation kernel is free on
\(q_g=gqg^{-1}\). Arbitrary lifts of the three evaluated bridges add
variables \(U,V,W\) in this free kernel. Semidirect-product expansion
gives an exact word \(n_3(U,V,W)\).

Literal liftability is equivalent to cyclic reduction of \(n_3\) being
one negative basis letter. The \(U=V=W=1\) lift has cyclic kernel length
seven, but arbitrary \(U,V,W\) remain open.

The first unresolved projection is the full Fox linearization

\[
N_{\mathrm{ab}}\cong\mathbb Z[G].
\]

Scalar augmentation already equals \(-1\), but the group-ring equation
has not been solved. Finite quotients and mod-\(p\) group algebras provide
rigorous necessary sieves before the nonabelian equation.
