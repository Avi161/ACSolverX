# AK(3) unchanged-primitive-first carrier design

Date: 2026-07-26

## Objective

Complete every carrier-edge order which deletes an unchanged primitive
carrier before the changed row at the six nonprimitive D-tail
checkpoints.

## Structural split

Let the edge be \(T\leftarrow TuS^\sigma u^{-1}\).

1. If the deleted primitive row is the retained source S, quotient
   naturality kills the conjugated factor for every relative conjugator
   u. The edge is exactly invisible after source-first deletion.
2. In the signed-cyclic carrier image, the only non-source choices are:
   delete D after an A--W edge, or delete W after an A--D edge.
   These reduce to rank-three survivor classifications.

Thus the source-first half is an unbounded theorem, while only the
non-source half needs a finite signed-cyclic certificate.

## Proven result

- all A--D/W-first quotients have no primitive survivor;
- in the A--W/D-first quotients, only the two
  \((\eta,\epsilon)=(-,+)\) Q-rows are primitive;
- deleting those Q-rows gives fixed-coordinate representatives
  classically equivalent to the known floor-23 corridor, with no
  endpoint of floor at most 12.

There are 2,928 non-source states: 2,184 have no primitive survivor and
744 have Q as their unique primitive survivor. The final pairs give 154
distinct normalized endpoints per target direction. Their minimum
floors are 19 and 14, respectively. In each endpoint Aut-orbit, the
fixed double-deletion-coordinate representative is a multiplication
by a conjugate, hence a short classical AC sequence, from the floor-23
baseline. Arbitrary straightener outputs are only claimed stably
equivalent.

## Boundary

Arbitrary relative conjugators are covered only when the deleted
primitive row is the retained source. The non-source deletion theorem
is restricted to signed cyclic representatives. Changed-row-first
orders are Result 50. Two row changes and changed checkpoints remain
outside scope.
