# AK(3) minimum-tail Fox coset design

Date: 2026-07-25

## Objective

Decide the first full abelianized free-kernel obstruction for the
repositioned minimum-tail candidate.

## Coefficient reduction

Write the Fox equation as

\[
\Xi=A_0+A_Uu+A_Vv+A_Ww
\]

over \(\mathbb Z[G]\). If

\[
P=\langle K,\gamma b\gamma^{-1}\rangle,
\]

then the \(v,w\) coefficients generate exactly

\[
I_P=\sum_{p\in P}(p-1)\mathbb Z[G].
\]

Hence Fox solvability reduces exactly to

\[
\pi_P(A_0)+\pi_P(A_U)u=-[Pg]
\]

in \(\mathbb Z[P\backslash G]\).

## Subgroup geometry

After conjugation, \(P\) is generated by two translation-length-two
elements with disjoint Bass--Serre axes at distance one. Tree ping-pong
gives \(P\cong F_2\), and the central projection is injective on \(P\).
Euler characteristic proves infinite index.

## Finite quotient

The quotient

\[
x\mapsto(1\,2\,3),
\qquad
t\mapsto(0\,1\,2\,3)
\]

maps \(G\) onto \(S_4\) and \(P\) onto
\(\operatorname{Stab}(3)\cong S_3\). In the four-coset module,

\[
\bar A_0=(0,-2,0,1),
\qquad
\bar A_U=(2,0,0,-2).
\]

The \(U\)-orbit spans twice the augmentation lattice, so only target
residues in the point stabilizer survive. The identity survives; this is
a restriction, not a nonlift certificate.
