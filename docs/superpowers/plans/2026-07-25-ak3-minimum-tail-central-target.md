**Goal:** Decide whether \(g=c^{-1}\) solves the exact Fox coset
equation.

**Architecture:** Factor the equation through \(1+L\), identify that
operator with HNN-tree incidence, then decide the residual augmentation
ideal using a second folded subgroup core.

**Tech Stack:** Markdown proof, pure Python, amalgam normal form,
free-product Stallings cores, Bass--Serre HNN tree, coset modules.

## Global Constraints

- Tree incidence arguments apply only to finite-support chains.
- Every left/right group-ring orientation must be explicit.
- No bounded search may be promoted to the theorem.
- No AC graph search.
- AK(3) remains open.
