> **For agentic workers:** use subagent-driven development task by task with
> independent mathematical and quality review.

**Goal:** Derive the exact crossed Fox--Magnus Hessian, pull it back through
the anchored tree-flow map, and force a finite-index-periodicity versus
infinite-Hankel-rank decision attempt on the actual source image.

static regularity to Markov closure, and no AC/stable-AC claim without an
independent move path or universal theorem.

## Acceptance criteria

- The AST and pinned table independently agree.
- First derivatives reproduce the existing operators exactly.
- The inverse-leaf Hessian is polarity-correct.
- Symbolic and direct four-corner wedges agree on independent fixtures.
- Symbolic and direct raw tensors agree before exterior projection, and a
  pinned complete-integer fixture reducing modulo two to `delta + q*delta`
  proves diagonals were not deleted early.
- Fixed section defects are excluded only from the cross Hessian, not unary
  syndrome terms.
- Tensor diagonals are propagated through nontrivial quotient payloads before
  exterior killing.
- The anchored formula is universal, not a depth bound.
- The actual rank target is the complete validity-plus-syndrome function,
  with explicit base/unary and diagonal-left evaluation interfaces.
- Any finite state is reversible and factors through a displayed finite
  quotient; any infinite-rank result lives inside the exact anchored image.
- Worktree and origin are synchronized, push history is logged append-only,
  and no proof/test process remains.
