normal form for \(P\backslash G\).

weight, then retain the central sign with a \(GL_2(\mathbb F_3)\)
representation and a separating row functional.

**Tech Stack:** Markdown proof, pure Python, amalgam normal form,
folded free-product core, finite-field linear algebra, `runpy`.

  unless separately proved.

  nonliftability certificate.
