# Hostile review: simultaneous ambient automorphisms and thickenability

1. **The signed-permutation claim is correct in both directions.** A basis permutation or inversion is induced by a cellular homeomorphism of the rose, and precomposing every attaching map with that graph homeomorphism gives a cellular homeomorphism of the exact presentation complexes. Relator permutation, cyclic change of a 2-cell basepoint, and 2-cell orientation reversal are likewise genuine homeomorphisms. No stable-AC or handlebody argument is needed for this subgroup.

2. **The memo correctly refuses to promote a Nielsen transvection to an exact-complex theorem.** Lackenby Section 2.2 defines move (5) by a literal free-semigroup substitution, and Theorem 6.3 identifies a slide in the first meridional system with that Nielsen move at the level of the presentation read from the Heegaard data. Neither statement says that the new canonical one-vertex occurrence-book complex is embedded. A handlebody homeomorphism preserves an embedded curve/disc system and changes a marking; replacing the moved spine by the fixed rose requires precisely the fold/retraction whose injectivity is missing. The local Neuwirth theorem tests the resulting occurrence book, not merely the represented free-group elements or a disjoint curve system.

3. **Semigroup spelling blocks the automatic reverse implication.** A chosen literal lift of a transvection replaces each occurrence separately. Applying a literal lift of the inverse automorphism recovers the original tuple only after free cancellations in general, not as the same exact word-realized CW complex. Lackenby's warning immediately before Lemma 6.5 is directly on point: after switching from free-semigroup words to free-group elements, thickenability is not immediately well-defined because different semigroup realizations might behave differently. Thus neither \(\phi^{-1}\) nor a handle slide supplies both directions without a separate cancellation/spine lemma.

4. **Stable ambient equivalence proves no thickenability transport.** Proposition 3.3 of `literature/proofs/AK3_DUAL_SOURCE_COMPRESSION.md` proves \(P\sim_{\mathrm{st}}\phi(P)\) for balanced trivial-group presentations by fresh-letter exchanges and substitution/removal. That is an algebraic stable-AC statement. Treating it as a thickenability invariant would contradict the deliberately one-sided scope of the repository's exact Neuwirth and thickenability results; those results recompute each exact complex and transport only positive stable-AC consequences, never negative thickenability verdicts.

5. **Formal 3-deformation does not fill the missing direction.** Lackenby Lemma 5.1 identifies \(Q^{**}\)-equivalence with 3-deformation, while Proposition 5.2 states only that two spines of one common 3-manifold regular neighbourhood are 3-deformation equivalent. The needed converse—an arbitrary 3-deformation, or each elementary expansion/collapse in it, preserves existence of a 3-thickening—is neither stated nor proved. The memo correctly says that this argument is insufficient; it does not make the invalid stronger claim that 3-deformation is known not to preserve thickenability.

6. **One passage needs tightening before approval.** Lines 77–105 call \(h\) a homeomorphism of the handlebody \(H\) and then say that “applying \(h\)” produces \(h(K_P)\). That expression is not defined unless \(h\) is extended over an ambient neighbourhood containing the 2-cell pages (or the ambient manifold is explicitly reglued). Moreover, realizing only the outer class determines the relator words only up to a common conjugation/based-marking choice, which matters for an exact semigroup spelling. Rephrase this paragraph conditionally—*even granting such an extension and a based lift, its embedded spine is the moved rose, and identifying it with the fixed rose requires a non-embedded fold*. This repair strengthens the stated gap and does not alter the conclusion.

7. **The ultimate scope is otherwise exact.** The sources establish full invariance only for signed permutations and establish stable-AC equivalence for all ambient automorphisms. They establish neither invariance nor a counterexample for freely reduced transvection images of the canonical presentation complex. Therefore the AK(3) negative may be propagated through actual cellular symmetries but must not be propagated through the full \(\operatorname{Aut}(F_2)\)-orbit; equally, the memo correctly does not claim that thickenability is non-invariant.
