# Augmented weighted-cut covariance for the first period-two unary ray

Date: 2026-07-29

## 0. Theorem status

**OPEN.**  The complete raw-event calculation below proves an exact
all-index four-corner formula for the proposed augmented diagonal covariance,
but it does not prove that the formula vanishes.  It also does not produce an
exact counterexample to augmented covariance, chamber collapse, or diagonal
cancellation.  Consequently neither weighted-cut identity

\[
 \mathscr C(a_{ij},d^j_{ij})=[i-j=0]+[i-j=1],
 \qquad
 \mathscr C(a_{ij},d^{\rm diag}_{ij})=0
\tag{0.1}
\]

is closed here.  The all-index edge laws and
\(u_{ij}=\delta_{ij}\) therefore remain open.

What is proved is the exact complete-sum covariance reduction

\[
\boxed{
 J_{i+1,j+1}+J_{ij}
 =\mathscr C(a,g)+\mathbb B(b,f)+\mathbb B(b,g)+\mathbb B(f,g),
}
\tag{0.2}
\]

where \(a,b,f,g\) are the four explicit actual-merged activity increments
defined in Section 4.  Formula (0.2) retains all sixteen correction
occurrences, the six old moving flow paths, all six translated increment paths,
negative-occurrence chronology, the raw quotient prefixes, the raw mirror
cochain \(\rho\), actual aggregated \(B+D\) coefficient parities, every
new--new quadratic term, and endpoint reversal.  It is an identity of the
literal raw-event model, not a central-label surrogate.

No index grid, representative census, broad search, or finite-sample
inference is used.  Section 7 contains one constant-size structural replay
performed only after (0.2) was derived; it is a semantic pin, not a proof of
any all-index vanishing.

## 2. One fixed token universe for the complete merged endpoint

Let \(\mathscr P\) contain two kinds of core tokens.

1. Every raw Schreier event in every fixed literal leaf of the expanded AST
   is a fixed token.  Its activity is one, its raw-mirror weight is zero,
   and its position is its literal AST/raw-letter position.
2. For every occurrence \(o\) and canonical module vertex \(v\), there is a
   correction token \(p=(o,v)\).  Its label and raw weight are

   \[
    \ell(o,v)=\tau_o(v):=\operatorname{cvert}(q_ov),
    \qquad r(o,v)=\rho_o(v).
   \tag{2.1}
   \]

The all-word raw bridge first freely reduces

\[
 q_ovccv^{-1}q_o^{-1}=zccz^{-1}.
\tag{2.2}
\]

If the first-half Schreier labels of \(z\) are
\(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_m\), including the bridge's terminal-`c`
subcase and `tau` deletion, then

\[
 \rho_o(v)=\sum_{h=1}^m[\alpha_h\ne\tau_o(v)]\pmod2.
\tag{2.3}
\]

Thus \(r\) is the literal raw mirror cochain.  It is not a function of the
central label: the already proved examples
\((q,v)=(\texttt{tc},\texttt{cT})\) and
\((q,v)=(\mathrm{eps},\mathrm{eps})\) have the same central label and raw
weights one and zero respectively.  That fact is used only to forbid an
invalid simplification; it is not used as a counterexample to the complete
covariance identity.

For a complete integral current \(C=B+D\), define the actual merged activity

\[
 a_C(o,v)=C_{s_o}(v)\pmod2,
\tag{2.4}
\]

and give every fixed token activity one.  Equation (2.4) is taken only after
all base, anchor, and flow contributions at \((s_o,v)\) have been aggregated
integrally.  Virtual base and direction tokens are never assigned separate
chronologies.

Let \(\triangleleft\) be the fixed token chronology: literal AST order,
raw-letter order inside a fixed leaf, increasing shortlex vertex order inside
a positive correction occurrence, and decreasing shortlex order inside a
negative occurrence.  For distinct tokens put

\[
 \xi(p,q)=
 \begin{cases}
 [\ell(p)<_{\rm sl}\ell(q)],&p\triangleleft q,\\
 [\ell(q)<_{\rm sl}\ell(p)],&q\triangleleft p.
 \end{cases}
\tag{2.5}
\]

This symmetric kernel records every chronological cut comparison.  Inserting
or deleting a merged support vertex never permutes the common vertices, so
there is no additional survivor-permutation term.

## 3. Exact endpoint quadratic form and endpoint reversal

For a finite activity \(a\) on \(\mathscr P\), with all fixed tokens active,
put

\[
 \Phi(a)=\sum_p a(p)r(p)
 +\sum_{\{p,q\}}a(p)a(q)\xi(p,q).
\tag{3.1}
\]

For \(C=B+D\) with \(D\) an integral homogeneous correction direction, the
residual \(\mathcal R(C)\) has zero linear Schreier coordinate.  On precisely
this zero-linear domain, the raw-mirror contraction and
matching-independent heterochromatic chord theorem prove

\[
 \Phi(a_C)=\phi_\infty(\mathcal R(C)).
\tag{3.2}
\]

For an increment \(d\), fixed tokens have \(d(p)=0\).  Define

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathbb B(u,v)
  &=\sum_{\{p,q\}}\xi(p,q)
    \bigl(u(p)v(q)+v(p)u(q)\bigr),\\
 Q(d)&=\sum_{\{p,q\}}d(p)d(q)\xi(p,q),\\
 \mathscr C(a,d)
  &=\Phi(a+d)+\Phi(a)\\
  &=\sum_pd(p)r(p)+\mathbb B(a,d)+Q(d).
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.3}
\]

All expressions are in \(\mathbb F_2\).  The last line visibly retains the
raw cochain, the load against the complete old merged activity, and the
new--new quadratic comparisons.

For a difference word
\(\mathcal R(C')\mathcal R(C)^{-1}\), assume
\(C=B+D\) and \(C'=B+D'\) with both \(D,D'\) integral homogeneous
directions.  Thus both endpoint residuals have zero linear Schreier
coordinate.  Use the disjoint endpoint involutions.  Reversing the second
endpoint preserves raw mirror crossings.
For the stable-sort part,

\[
 \operatorname{sort}(\operatorname{rev}\mathcal K)
 +\operatorname{sort}(\mathcal K)
 =\sum_{x<y}m_xm_y=0,
\tag{3.4}
\]

because every core-label multiplicity \(m_x\) is even.  There are no
cross-endpoint chords.  Hence endpoint reversal gives exactly

\[
 \phi_\infty(\mathcal R(C')\mathcal R(C)^{-1})
 =\Phi(a_{C'})+\Phi(a_C)=\mathscr C(a_C,a_{C'}+a_C).
\tag{3.5}
\]

No emitted label, negative raw sign, or inverse endpoint has been discarded
in passing to (3.5).

## 5. Complete augmented covariance identity

The unshifted and shifted \(j\)-cuts are

\[
 J_{ij}=\mathscr C(a,b),
 \qquad
 J_{i+1,j+1}=\mathscr C(a+f,b+g).
\tag{5.1}
\]

Use (3.3), bilinearity of \(\mathbb B\), and

\[
 Q(b+g)+Q(b)=Q(g)+\mathbb B(b,g).
\tag{5.2}
\]

Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
 J_{i+1,j+1}+J_{ij}
 &=\mathscr C(a+f,b+g)+\mathscr C(a,b)\\
 &=\underbrace{\mathscr C(a,g)}_{\substack{
     \sum g\rho+\mathbb B(a,g)+Q(g)}}
   +\mathbb B(b,f)+\mathbb B(b,g)+\mathbb B(f,g).
\end{aligned}
\tag{5.3}
\]

This proves (0.2) for all \(i,j\ge0\).  Formula (5.3) is the exact
cochain/coboundary boundary of the proposed covariance:

\[
\boxed{
 J_{i+1,j+1}=J_{ij}
 \iff
 \mathscr C(a,g)
 =\mathbb B(b,f)+\mathbb B(b,g)+\mathbb B(f,g).
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

The raw mirror contribution to the left side of (5.4) is exactly

\[
 \delta_g\rho=\sum_p g(p)r(p).
\tag{5.5}
\]

All other raw mirror terms occur twice and cancel.  Every chronological cut
comparison, including negative occurrences, is in the four displayed
\(\mathbb B/Q\) terms.  Every old actual merged \(B+D\) coefficient parity is
in \(\mathbb B(a,g)\), and all new--new terms are in
\(Q(g),\mathbb B(b,f),\mathbb B(b,g),\mathbb B(f,g)\).

Equation (5.4) is not proved to hold.  The existing unique-tree-flow theorem
proves (4.7), but it gives no identity between the raw cochain (5.5) and the
shortlex cut kernels on the right of (5.4).  Central-label covariance cannot
supply that identity because \(\rho_o(v)\) is not determined by
\(\tau_o(v)\).  Nor does quadraticity force it: the nonzero four-source
toggle \(g\) leaves the genuinely unary term \(\mathscr C(a,g)\).

There is a useful consistency identity at the scalar level.  If

\[
 \mathcal D_{ij}=\mathscr C(a_{ij},d^{\rm diag}_{ij})
 =u_{i+1,j+1}+u_{ij},
\tag{5.6}
\]

then

\[
 J_{i+1,j+1}+J_{ij}=\mathcal D_{i,j+1}+\mathcal D_{ij}.
\tag{5.7}
\]

Thus augmented covariance is equivalent to constancy of the diagonal defect
as \(j\) increases with \(i\) fixed.  This is a consistency check on (5.3),
not a proof that either side vanishes.

## 6. Chamber and diagonal consequences, conditional only

If (5.4) is proved, \(J_{ij}\) is constant under
\((i,j)\mapsto(i+1,j+1)\), hence depends only on \(d=i-j\).  It then remains
to prove, by genuine cyclic all-power normal forms at the initial point of
each diagonal ray,

\[
 J(d)=
 \begin{cases}
 0,&d<0,\\
 1,&d=0,1,\\
 0,&d>1.
 \end{cases}
\tag{6.1}
\]

For \(d\ge0\) the initial point is \((d,0)\); for \(d<0\) it is
\((0,-d)\).  The primitive proof for
\(P_{ij}=\delta_{ij}\) legitimately splits an all-power word comparison
into a diagonal, a longer-side chamber, and a common-prefix chamber.  It
does not prove (6.1): the complete cut contains the raw weights, all sixteen
occurrences, all six flow paths, base load, and the new--new terms in (5.4).
No chamber table for these complete terms is supplied here.

Likewise, the zero diagonal-step target remains

\[
 \mathcal D_{ij}=\mathscr C(a_{ij},d^{\rm diag}_{ij})=0.
\tag{6.2}
\]

If augmented covariance is first proved, (5.7) reduces (6.2) to the
one-parameter boundary family

\[
 \mathcal D_{i,0}=0\qquad(i\ge0),
\tag{6.3}
\]

because \(\mathcal D_{i,j+1}=\mathcal D_{ij}\).  Equation (6.3), not one
index cell, is then the smallest remaining diagonal target.  It still
requires an all-power normal-form proof.
