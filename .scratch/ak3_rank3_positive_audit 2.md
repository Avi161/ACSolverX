# Rank-three Neuwirth positive audit: rejected by exact sign provenance

## Verdict

The two apparent genus-zero presentations

```text
S1 = TTX | TZX | TTTzzzz
S2 = TZX | ZZX | TTTzzzz
```

are **not** one-move neighbours of the MMS02 search state `P*`.  They are
one-move neighbours of a different presentation obtained by changing the
last letter of the first relator from `x` to `X`.

The positive rotation data for `S1` and `S2` are internally consistent, but
they have no proved path from AK(3).  They therefore cannot trigger a
regular-neighbourhood encoder, Regina, Lackenby's theorem, or an AC/stable-AC
claim.

## Exact sign audit

The committed bridge record gives

```text
P* = ZYx | ZxyX | ZZZyyyy
```

with a lowercase final `x` in the first row.  Under the stated renaming

```text
old x -> x, old y -> z, old z -> t
```

letter signs are preserved, so the exact image is

```text
P*_actual = TZx | TxzX | TTTzzzz.
```

The input used for the apparent positives was instead

```text
P*_wrong = TZX | TxzX | TTTzzzz.
```

`TZx` and `TZX` are not cyclic conjugates or inverse-cyclic conjugates.  This
is not a display normalization: it changes the exponent of `x` by two.

## Complete one-move provenance check

The checker independently enumerates every move

```text
ri <- rot(ri) rot(rj^epsilon),  i != j, epsilon in {+1,-1},
```

followed only by free/cyclic reduction, row inversion/cyclic normalization,
and row permutation.  These normalizations expand into primitive AC1--AC3
moves.

For `P*_actual`, the complete one-move shell has 172 distinct states.  Its
total-length histogram is

```text
15: 11, 16: 11, 17: 33, 18: 47, 19: 14, 21: 56.
```

Thus every genuine one-move neighbour has total length at least 15.  Neither
length-13 tuple `S1` nor `S2` occurs.  Repeating the same complete enumeration
from `P*_wrong` contains both of them, locating the error exactly at the
uppercase substitution.

## Why no encoder or Regina record is produced

A marked regular-neighbourhood triangulation of `S1` or `S2` would validate
only those wrong-ancestry complexes.  It would not create an AC path from
AK(3), and an arbitrary unmarked ball would validate nothing.  The mandatory
incidence/Regina gate is therefore intentionally not entered after provenance
failure.

The negative Neuwirth values for `P*_actual` and `P*_wrong` are local facts
about those exact word-realized complexes.  They are not AC invariants and
give no obstruction to AK(3).
