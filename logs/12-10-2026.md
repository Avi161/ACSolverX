## 12:48:47 UTC · `ec5dde8`

Rank-3 baseline, cap counters. Not yet run: this commit goes to a small AWS box for its tests first. The two searches (`solvern.search_n` and its fast twin) now return three numbers per search: `cap_dropped`, the children that were not pushed because one of their relators is longer than the cap; `cap_dropped_min_total`, the smallest total length among them; and `cap_first_bind`, the number of the first pop at which a child dropped earlier had a heap key (total length, depth) no larger than the popped state's. The heap only holds pushed states, so a search with no such pop is pop for pop the search at any larger cap.

The runners store the three numbers for every search: as fields of the control, relator-subword and sampled rows, in `<stem>_L<n>_capcount.npz` beside each word-length array, and inside the rank-4/5 arrays. The verifier checks them against each other, against the node counts and against the reference search; `cap_exhaustion.py` tabulates them; `cap_compare.py` fails if a search with no bind at the smaller cap differs at the larger one.
