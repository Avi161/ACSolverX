## 07:0x UTC · The opening idea dies, and the theorem that kills it

Positive ladder: 63 AC-trivial γ_N = 0 bases, fully triangulated to ranks 3–9 — γ_N = 0

**Theorem S3.** A chord refinement adds a 1-cell *drawn inside* an existing 2-cell, so
`K_{P′}` is a **subdivision** of `K_P` and `|K_{P′}| ≅ |K_P|`. Triangulation changes nothing
topological. Verified by hand on AK(3) (triangle `aYX` glued to pentagon `axYXY` along `a`
reads back `xyxYXY`).

Also caught my own unit error: `minimum_defect` is `2·γ_N` in this repo. AK(3) is
`minimum_defect` 4 = γ_N 2 at rank 2 **and** at rank 9 — unchanged, as the theorem predicts,
not "2 → 4" as I first wrote.

## 08:0x UTC · S12 — the method, and the honest answer to the brief

`S12_CERTIFICATE_HUNT.md`: **Stable Certificate Hunt** — stabilize `k` times, search AC1–AC3
at rank `n+k`, decide orientable thickenability at each state, cash any hit through Lackenby
Thm 1.3. Sound (proof in §2); a semi-algorithm; not a reduction to another open problem
because thickenability is decidable.

Corrected the audit's trap T-S9, which had the gap on the wrong side: an orientable PL
3-manifold *is* a 3-manifold, so **a hit is not encumbered by Joint-A at all**; the
orientability gap bites only on **nulls** — including the 0/171,842 depth-1 AK(3) harvest.

**The answer to the session's question, as it stands:** going to 9 or 10 generators does not
help *by any mechanism* — abbreviation is a provable no-op and splitting is monotone. It
helps by a **population effect** (certificates are commoner at higher rank) and a
**computability effect** (the decidable test is affordable at lengths where rank 2 is not).
That argues for the hunt at **rank 4–6**, not rank 2 and not rank 9.
