# Lessons — 2026-08-28

### Ray polarization does not control fixed exterior torsion

[TRAP] The complete external occurrence group-ring area was identified with the entire homogeneous coinvariant defect by citing a theorem proved only on torsion-free ray coordinates. Mixed polarization can determine those free coordinates while missing inversion-fixed `Z/2` values from same-occurrence inverse-jet or canonical-section terms.

[WORKS] State the group-ring area first as an exact ledger for the strict external pairs `o<p`. Promote it to the full homogeneous `Lambda^2 M` defect only after a separate tensor-level proof evaluates every same-occurrence term before coinvariants; never infer that lift from ray-reader cancellation.

### Use the destination header for long theorem insertions

[TRAP] A long theorem insertion failed because its patch anchor repeated the preceding conclusion with a different formatter line wrap.

[WORKS] When inserting a self-contained section, anchor only on the exact destination section header and replace that header with the new section plus the unchanged header.

### Canonical quotient equality does not lift literally

[TRAP] Assuming that $q\widetilde v$ and $\widetilde{qv}$ differ only by the cyclic stabilizer led to the false identity $q r_vq^{-1}=r_{qv}$.  The free-group lift has an $N$-valued terminal-normalization defect.

[WORKS] Use the exact transport identity (5.41), $q_o r_vq_o^{-1}=k_zr_xk_z^{-1}$, and carry the resulting $n[k_z,r_x]$ term through $\Lambda^2M$ before asserting that same-occurrence torsion cancels.

### Open occurrence tensors are not exterior classes

[TRAP] Projecting each correction occurrence to a wedge before summing lost the inverse-square and orientation terms.  One occurrence has nonzero relation-module value, so its tensor is not yet a class in $\gamma_2N/\gamma_3N$.

[WORKS] Keep canonical-section, inverse, bridge, and external terms in $M\otimes M$ until the complete zero-linear aggregate is diagonal-free and antisymmetric; only then apply the ordered-half wedge reader.

### Literal lifts differ from semidirect section lifts

[TRAP] Replacing an actual occurrence lift $\widehat q_o$ by the multiplicative lift $\widehat m_oc^{\eta_o}$ silently removed a fixed $N$-valued prefix defect, even though both words represent the same quotient element.

[WORKS] Insert $\lambda_o=\widehat q_o(\widehat m_oc^{\eta_o})^{-1}\in N$ and carry its current $P_o=\overline{\lambda_o}$ through every terminal bridge.  Only the remaining universal odd-prefix derivation may be collapsed across occurrences.

### Prefix and vertex sections need separate comparisons

[TRAP] Correcting the literal prefix lift still left an unsound substitution of the multiplicative lift $j(a)$ for the fixed Schreier representative $\widetilde a$ in $r_a=\widetilde a c^2\widetilde a^{-1}$.

[WORKS] If the canonical right-$H$ representative lifts $ac^{\rho(a)}$, insert $\mu(a)=\widetilde a(j(a)c^{\rho(a)})^{-1}$ at the source and the inverse comparison at the destination.  The terminal current contains both the prefix offset $P_o$ and the vertex-section coboundary $C_o(a)$ in addition to the universal crossed derivation.

### A right-coset representative is not a group representative

[TRAP] Under the implementation's `c_vertex`, the canonical word for the coset $aH$ may represent $ac$ rather than $a$ in $Q$; for example a terminal $c$ is removed.  Treating it as a lift of $a$ made the comparison factor fail to lie in $N$.

[WORKS] Introduce the unique terminal bit $\rho(a)$ with quotient value $ac^{\rho(a)}$.  Compare $\widetilde a$ with $j(a)c^{\rho(a)}$ and use centralization of $c^2$ to pass to the Schreier-generator identity before abelianizing.

### Declare reduced literal lifts before using them

[TRAP] A formula for the prefix current $P_o$ used that $\widehat q_o$ was the literal spelling of the quotient-reduced prefix, while the theorem had only called it a chosen lift.

[WORKS] When a later reduction argument depends on the spelling of a lift, declare at its first definition that the pinned quotient-reduced word is interpreted as the identical letter string in the free group.

### A transport conjugator is unique only modulo its target generator

[TRAP] Two exact factorizations of $q r_aq^{-1}$ were treated as having the same conjugator current.  Their conjugators can differ by any power of the target basis generator $r_x$.

[WORKS] Use the free-group centralizer $C_N(r_x)=\langle r_x\rangle$ and state current identities modulo $\mathbb Z e_x$.  This ambiguity is harmless only after checking that it cancels in $K\otimes e_x-e_x\otimes K$ and in every nontrivial double-coset coordinate.

### Recheck the branch immediately before every commit

[TRAP] The shared worktree was switched from `codex/proofs` to `main` by another process after staging and before the commit command.  The commit was rejected, and the branch switch had automatically preserved the staged proof/log changes in `stash@{0}`.

### Target-incident mixed branches do not cover all mixed terms

[TRAP] Solving a paired-bucket collision with one token based at the target source gives four branches for the second source, but this does not control a mixed term whose two tokens come from two different non-target cells.  The latter retains one free source parameter.

[WORKS] Before claiming bounded degree for a clustered collision graph, partition mixed terms into target-incident and non-target pairs.  Use the full three-source equation $wH=h^{-1}vc^\delta u^{-1}g^\epsilon uH$ for the second class and keep the free source $v$ explicit.

[WORKS] When converting an extremal bound into a finite word alphabet, use the literal derived radius from the localization theorem.  Define the forest ball with $L_{\mathrm{end}}(E)$ unless a separate inequality proving $E\ge L_{\mathrm{end}}(E)$ has been established.

### Use fixed-string search for TeX fragments
