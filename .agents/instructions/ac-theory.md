# AC and stable-AC theory

Treat computations, bounded searches, failed preflights, and intermediate theorems as evidence with their stated scope, never as an Andrews--Curtis or stable-AC resolution. The final goal is a correct proof-resolution result; do not close a requested proof-resolution loop on a bounded negative or an honest partial result unless an external blocker genuinely prevents continuation.

State hypotheses, move classes, orientations, and quotient scope exactly. Independently replay word identities and proof obligations; do not promote advisor formulas, scratch numerics, or heuristic output into a theorem. A certificate verifier must be independent of the search or generator it checks.

Obtain a read-only Sol xhigh APPROVE/REVISE/BLOCK review for every substantive proof or plan slice and reconcile objections against evidence. Use Sol ultra only for a final theorem claim, long-experiment authorization, or unresolved xhigh soundness disagreement.
