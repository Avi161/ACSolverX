# A second source escape meets a mod-three cyclic obstruction

## Status

There is a 12-entry \(L_1\)-coupled homogeneous syzygy which is new modulo
2 and kills all four previously certified mod-two degree-two functionals.
Nevertheless its nonlinear lift is globally obstructed at degree two by the
same cyclic three-point quotient over \(\mathbb F_3\).

This provides a fifth independent search direction, not a solution of the
period-two lift or the original free-group equation.

## 1. The exact syzygy

Retain
\[
Q=\langle c,t\mid c^2=1\rangle,
\qquad
M=\mathbb Z[Q/\langle c\rangle],
\]
write \(T=t^{-1}\), and let \(e_q\) be the \(C\)-vertex basis.

Define \(v=(v_0,\ldots,v_4)\in M^5\) by
\[
\begin{aligned}
v_0={}&0,\\
v_1={}&e_{TT},\\
v_2={}&e_{TT}-e_{cTT},\\
v_3={}&-e_{TTctcT}-e_{TTctcTcT}+e_{TTctcTctcTT},\\
v_4={}&e_T+e_{TT}-e_{cTT}+e_{TctcT}+e_{TctcTcT}
        -e_{TctcTctcTT}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
It has 12 support entries, coefficient \(\ell^1\)-norm 12, and component
augmentations
\[
(0,1,0,-1,2).
\tag{2}
\]

Exact sparse expansion gives
\[
\sum_{i=0}^4L_iv_i=0,
\tag{3}
\]
with component image statistics
\[
(0,0),(4,4),(4,4),(6,6),(10,10).
\tag{4}
\]
Seven entries lie outside the original one-hop variable set:
\[
\begin{gathered}
(2,TT),\ (2,cTT),\ (3,TTctcTcT),\ (3,TTctcTctcTT),\\
(4,TT),\ (4,cTT),\ (4,TctcTctcTT).
\end{gathered}
\tag{5}
\]

## 2. Forest construction

Use the free forest basis
\[
A=t,
\qquad B=X,
\qquad G=U^{-1}t^{-1},
\]
and lower-case letters for inverses. The \(L_1e_{TT}\) source boundary has
sum zero on both exact \(K=\langle A,B,G\rangle\)-orbits. Its two tree
paths are
\[
e_1\xrightarrow{\ aaBgA\ }e_{ctcTctcTT},
\qquad
e_{ctcT}\xrightarrow{\ aGaGbA\ }e_{tcTT}.
\tag{6}
\]
The letters are successive left actions, read left-to-right. Converting the
edges of (6) through
\[
L_2=1-B,
\qquad L_3=(G-1)t,
\qquad L_4=A-1
\]
reconstructs (1) exactly and proves (3) independently of sparse collection.

## 3. Four mod-two escapes

Add \(v\) to the canonical eight-entry first-layer solution and replay the
complete nonlinear free-group recurrence. The exact residual data are

| quantity | value |
|---|---:|
| free residual length | 276 |
| Schreier-kernel length | 64 |
| relation-module residual terms | 0 |
| degree-two wedge support | 79 |
| degree-two coefficient \(\ell^1\)-norm | 94 |

The full wedge sum is \(-24\). The nontrivial three-point coordinates are
\[
(-4,3,3),
\tag{7}
\]
the four-point coordinates are
\[
(2,3,1,2,0,2),
\tag{8}
\]
and the cyclic three-point coordinates are
\[
(-1,-3,-4).
\tag{9}
\]
Every coordinate sum in (7)--(9), and the full sum, is even. Therefore
\[
(\Phi_\infty,\Phi_3,\Phi_4,\Psi_{\rm cyc}^{(2)})=(0,0,0,0).
\tag{10}
\]

The preceding certificate enumerated all 16 mod-two classes in the affine
span generated by \(u_0,u_1,w_0,z\), and every class had a nonzero four-bit
vector. Equation (10) therefore proves that \(v\) is outside that known
four-direction span modulo 2.

## 4. The mod-three separator

Use the same cyclic action
\[
c=1,
\qquad t=(0\ 1\ 2)
\tag{11}
\]
on three points, now over \(\mathbb F_3\). The coordinate-sum functional on
\(\Lambda^2\mathbb F_3[\{0,1,2\}]\) is not invariant because wedge
orientation signs survive at odd primes. In the ordered basis
\((01),(02),(12)\), the correct invariant covector is
\[
\Psi_{\rm cyc}^{(3)}(a_{01},a_{02},a_{12})
=a_{01}-a_{02}+a_{12}.
\tag{12}
\]
Indeed \(t=(0\ 1\ 2)\) sends the three basis wedges to
\((12),-(01),-(02)\), and the covector in (12) is fixed by the dual
signed action. Since
\[
\varepsilon(L_0),\ldots,\varepsilon(L_4)=(0,0,0,0,0),
\]
it annihilates every possible second-layer correction.

The exact projected residual is still (9), and its signed value is
\[
-1-(-3)+(-4)=-2\equiv1\pmod3.
\tag{13}
\]
Thus the 79-term degree-two residual is not in
\[
\sum_{i=0}^4L_i(\Lambda^2M).
\tag{14}
\]
The induced operator image has rank two over \(\mathbb F_3\); adjoining
(9) raises the rank to three. This proves global nonmembership for this
lift, with no restriction on second-layer support.

## 5. Consequence and frontier

The two source-coupled directions expose a prime hierarchy:

- \(z_1=e_{ct}\) defeats the first three mod-two bits but is detected by the
  cyclic action modulo 2;
- \(v_1=e_{TT}\) defeats all four mod-two bits but is detected by the same
  cyclic action and its signed invariant covector modulo 3.

This does not by itself close integer combinations of the five known
directions: coefficients at different primes must be combined before a new
direction is demanded. The subsequent five-direction certificate performs
that classification. A new \(S_3\)-type three-point action separates every
mod-four coefficient class in the known span, so only after that result is a
direction outside the five-dimensional span necessary.
