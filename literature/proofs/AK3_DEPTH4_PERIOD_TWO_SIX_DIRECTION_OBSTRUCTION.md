# A two-point quotient closes the known six-direction span

## Status

There is a sixth exact source-coupled homogeneous direction outside the
previous five-dimensional span. It defeats all five existing mod-two
functionals, and an integral combination also defeats the signed mod-three
functional. A new two-point quotient detects that combination and, together
with the earlier functionals, obstructs every integer coefficient class in
the known six-direction span.

This is not a global classification of the full homogeneous syzygy module.
The period-two lift and the original free-group equation remain open.

## 1. The sixth direction

Write \(T=t^{-1}\). Define \(s=(s_0,\ldots,s_4)\) by
\[
\begin{aligned}
s_0={}&0,\\
s_1={}&e_{tcTTT},\\
s_2={}&-e_{cT}+e_{cTT}+e_{tcTTT}-e_{cttcTTT},\\
s_3={}&-e_{TTcT}+e_{TTctcTcT}-e_{TTcttcTTT}\\
      &{}+e_{TTctcTctctcTTT}-e_{TTctcTctttcTTT},\\
s_4={}&e_{TcT}+e_{tcTTT}-e_{ctcTTT}+e_{ttcTTT}-e_{TctcTcT}\\
      &{}+e_{TcttcTTT}-e_{TctcTctctcTTT}+e_{TctcTctttcTTT}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
It has 18 support entries and coefficient \(\ell^1\)-norm 18. Exact sparse
expansion gives
\[
\sum_{i=0}^4L_i s_i=0,
\tag{2}
\]
with component image statistics
\[
(0,0),(4,4),(8,8),(10,10),(14,14).
\tag{3}
\]

In the forest basis \(A=t,B=X,G=U^{-1}t^{-1}\), the two source paths are
\[
\begin{aligned}
tttcTTT&\xrightarrow{\ aaBgA\ }ctcTctctcTTT,\\
ctcTctttcTTT&\xrightarrow{\ aGbaGBgAbaGA\ }tctcTTT.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
Letters are successive left actions, read left-to-right. Converting (4)
through \(L_2,L_3,L_4\) reconstructs (1), providing a second exact proof
of (2).

Adding \(s\) to the canonical solution gives a residual of free length 608,
kernel length 88, wedge support 165, and wedge coefficient norm 196. All
five existing mod-two functionals vanish, while the signed cyclic mod-three
functional has value 2. Direct support flattening gives
\[
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}(u_0,u_1,w_0,z,v)=5,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}(u_0,u_1,w_0,z,v,s)=6.
\tag{5}
\]
Thus \(s\) is genuinely outside the previous span.

## 3. The two-point separator

Over \(\mathbb F_2\), use the action on \(\{0,1\}\)
\[
c=1,
\qquad t=(0\ 1),
\tag{7}
\]
with base point 0. The wedge space is one-dimensional, generated by
\(e_0\wedge e_1\). Its coordinate functional is \(Q\)-invariant in
characteristic two. Since every \(L_i\) has augmentation zero, all five
induced operator maps are zero.

The residual from (6) maps exactly to
\[
(45).
\tag{8}
\]
Hence it is nonzero modulo two. The operator image has rank zero and
adjoining (8) raises the rank to one. Therefore (6) does not lift through
degree two.

## 4. Exhaustion of the six-direction integer span

Let \(a\in\mathbb Z^6\) be the coefficients of
\((u_0,u_1,w_0,z,v,s)\). The six degree-two obstruction bits are
integer-valued quadratic functions of \(a\). Modulo two, each has period
dividing four in every variable.

The exact certificate determines the complete quadratic model using
\[
1+2\cdot6+\binom62=28
\]
nonlinear replays: the origin, each \(e_i\), each \(2e_i\), and every
\(e_i+e_j\). It then verifies the model on 21 additional points, including
all \(3e_i\) and \(3e_i+e_j\) cases.

Evaluating the certified model on all
\[
4^6=4096
\tag{9}
\]
coefficient classes gives no zero obstruction vector for
\[
(\Phi_\infty,\Phi_3,\Phi_4,
  \Psi_{\rm cyc}^{(2)},\Phi_{S_3},\Phi_2).
\tag{10}
\]
The deterministic table hash is
\[
\texttt{58994ebbd8531bc402c4c8e15410c2a70448db8f8f50298f24f9c87fc1811298}.
\tag{11}
\]
Thus every integer combination in the known six-dimensional affine family
is globally obstructed at degree two by at least one of the six mod-two
functionals.

## 5. Consequence and frontier

The source-flow hierarchy has advanced twice without becoming global:

- the fifth direction escaped four mod-two bits and met an odd-prime
  obstruction;
- the sixth direction escaped five mod-two bits, while \(\Phi_2\) closes
  its entire integer span.

Any actual degree-two lift must use a homogeneous first-layer direction
outside the known six-dimensional span. The exact Stallings-cover rewrite
now constructs every balanced single-column \(L_1e_q\) flow without radius
search. Through word depth eight, 165 such columns were checked; only the
sixth direction killed the first five mod-two bits, and it was caught by the
signed mod-three functional. This remains bounded evidence, not a theorem
about all source columns.

The next theoretical target is a finite-state description of the obstruction
quadratics under exact subgroup rewriting, or a global cokernel argument for
the complete source-coupled syzygy module.
