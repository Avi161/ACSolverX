## Why the aut-min side climbs

Look at moves 17 to 23. Both sides are doing the same thing over and over,
peeling one factor off the second relator:

| | move 17 | move 20 | move 23 |
|---|---|---|---|
| original | `(Y, YYYYYYX)` | `(Y, YYYX)` | `(Y, X)` |
| aut-min | `(YXX, YXXYXXYXXYXXYXXYXXX)` | `(YXX, YXXYXXYXXX)` | `(X, YXX)` |

The original strips one letter at a time. The aut-min side strips one
**`YXX` block** at a time, because that is what `phi` turns a single
`y` into. Same computation, letters three times as long.

That is the whole mechanism. `phi` is a change of coordinates chosen to make
**one point** short, the starting pair. It is applied to every other point on
the route as well, and there it inflates rather than shrinks. The trade is
measurable across all 40 originals:

| over the 40 originals | starting length | peak length along the path |
|---|---:|---:|
| original, median | 25 | 29 |
| aut-min, median | **19** | **49** |

Aut-minimising buys a shorter start on 34 of 40 rows and pays
for it with a higher peak on **40 of 40** -- every one. The peak
ratio runs from 1.19 to 3.08, median 1.93.

## Why a higher peak is an exponentially worse search

The arms are best-first on length: `greedy`'s priority IS the total length,
and `s20_mk2`'s leads with it. Such a search pops states in roughly increasing
length, so to find a path whose peak is `P` it must be willing to work through
the states below `P`. The number of cyclically reduced words of length `n` in
F2 grows like `3^n`, so the territory is exponential in the peak.

The median row's peak rises by 24 letters when you
aut-minimise. That is a factor of about `3^24`, roughly
9e+10, in the size of the region a length-guided
search has to cover. Against that, the gap between 509 nodes and a failure at
10,000,000 is unsurprising.

This is the precise sense in which a **shorter presentation is a harder**
problem here. Short means sitting at the bottom of the length landscape, and
the exit is over a ridge.

## What this does not show

It does not show the aut-min pair has no short route of its own. It shows that
**this** route, the one inherited from the original, is a bad one for it. Some
other path might stay low the whole way. Nobody has found one: that is exactly
what 10,000,000 nodes failed to do, and the transported certificate is an upper
bound, not a distance.
