# AC19 residue at 10M nodes (cap 64), per arm

The 10M stage of the AC19 ladder: each arm re-ran its own unsolved-at-5M
rows at a 10,000,000-node budget, cap 64, into its own jsonl. Row lists
generated by `experiments/search/make_ac19_10m_lists.py` from the two 5M
jsonls (never edited by hand); `--check` re-derives them and passes
against this archive. Engine `hcompact`, `engine_mem_gen` 5, campaign box
743 GB / 96 threads, 5 lanes, reservation floor 214 states per popped
node. `CAMPAIGN=ac19_10m`, RUNBOOK section 8.

## Files

- `ac19_10m_greedy_b10000000_mrl64.jsonl` — the greedy arm, COMPLETE:
  31/31 rows, **3 solved / 28 exhausted**. The three solves carry their
  full paths and moves.
- `ac19_10m_s20_mk2_b10000000_mrl64.jsonl` — the s20_mk2 arm
  (priority = L + 20·S + 2·MK), COMPLETE: 9/9 rows, **0 solved**.
- `ac19_10m_results.csv` — the 40 row-runs as one table, one row per
  (name, arm). Verified field-for-field against both jsonls: `solved`,
  `nodes_explored`, `path_length`, `min_relator_length`, `min_r1`/`min_r2`,
  `seconds`, `peak_rss_gb`, the length columns and the `reduced` flag all
  agree, and no CSV row lacks a jsonl record.

No `run.log` was delivered for this stage. If it turns up it belongs here
beside the jsonls, as `run_log_ac19_10m.log`.

## Final tally (campaign complete, 40/40 row-runs)

| arm | rows | solved | exhausted | row time | peak RSS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| greedy | 31 | **3** | 28 | 16.14 core-h | 49.4–92.3 GB |
| s20_mk2 | 9 | **0** | 9 | 6.04 core-h | 90.6–108.2 GB |
| total | 40 | 3 | 37 | 22.18 core-h | 108.2 GB max |

Every row ran to its full 10,000,000-node budget. No error records, no
crashes, and **no row died at reservation exhaustion**, so the second
pass at `STATES_PER_NODE=236` that RUNBOOK section 8 provisioned for was
not needed. Peak RSS across all 40 rows was 108.15 GB against a 133.6 GiB
allocation-backed worst per lane and a 143.6 GiB child `RLIMIT_AS` — the
214 floor held with about 25% headroom, and five lanes peaked together at
roughly 541 GB of the box's 743 GB.

## The three solves buy no new mathematics

All three greedy solves are rows **s20_mk2 had already solved at a much
smaller budget**, recorded in `UNSOLVED_AFTER_5M.md` before this stage ran:

| row | greedy @10M | prior solve | ratio |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| `ac19_15866` | 8,204,360 nodes, path 65 | s20_mk2 @100k, cap 48: 17,369 nodes, path 96 | 472× |
| `ac19_44158` | 8,009,273 nodes, path 71 | s20_mk2 @100k, cap 48: 23,977 nodes, path 63 | 334× |
| `ac19_14060` | 5,309,101 nodes, path 136 | s20_mk2 @1M, cap 48: 181,300 nodes, path 136 | 29× |

A solved presentation stays solved, so the AC-status of no presentation
changed at this stage. **The residue unsolved by every arm at every budget
is the same 9 rows it was after 5M**: `ac19_16286`, `ac19_27254`,
`ac19_28131`, `ac19_44381`, `ac19_50841`, `ac19_51034`, `ac19_59576`,
`ac19_65753`, `ac19_7284`. What the stage bought is greedy catching up,
at 29× to 472× the node cost, on ground the other arm already held.

Each of the three needed more than 5,000,000 nodes (5.31M, 8.01M, 8.20M),
so they are genuine second-half finds rather than rows the 5M stage should
have caught — the doubling did produce solves in the new band. It did not
produce new results.

## The residue is smaller than the row count

Canonicalising each exhausted greedy row's `min_relator` collapses the 28
onto **18 distinct minimal presentations**:

| rows | minimal presentation | total |
|---:|---|---:|
| 4 | `YYXXXYxx` / `YYXYYxxyXYX` | 19 |
| 4 | `YYXXyxx` / `YYYxYXYYX` | 16 |
| 3 | `YYXXXXyx` / `YXXyXXXYx` | 17 |
| 2 | `YYXXXXyXX` / `YXYxxyxxyXX` | 20 |
| 2 | `YYXXyxx` / `YYYxYYxYX` | 16 |
| 1 each | 13 further presentations | 17–19 |

Three of those groups land on a presentation that **is the starting row of
another member of the same list** — `ac19_28131` (reached also by 16286
and 59576), `ac19_65206` (also by 20270, 36350, 57992) and `ac19_23156`
(also by 15507, 28510, 61253). Those rows are searching the same problem
from different entry points and failing at the same place. The nine-row
mutual residue similarly covers only 7 distinct minimal presentations.

Of the 28 exhausted greedy rows, 17 found something shorter than their
start and 11 never improved on it; of the 9 s20_mk2 rows, 4 and 5.
